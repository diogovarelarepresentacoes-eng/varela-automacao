Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Data we collect

When you connect TikTok, we receive your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and OAuth access/refresh tokens, and the creator information needed to publish (e.g. available privacy options). We also store the videos, captions and scheduling settings you submit.

2. How we use data

Solely to authenticate you and to upload and publish the content you choose to your own TikTok account, and to show you publishing status. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored on our private servers with access restricted to the operator. Tokens are stored encrypted and used only for the requested actions.

4. Sharing

Content is transmitted only to the platforms you select (TikTok, Instagram) through their official APIs. We do not share data with other third parties except when required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect at any time by revoking access in TikTok settings or by emailing us; we delete your tokens and associated data within 30 days of the request.

6. Your rights (LGPD)

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).
